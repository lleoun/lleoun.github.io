Current work

Gymkana Digital Turística, since 2022

Gymkana Digital Turística builds digital products for towns and tourist destinations.

Gymkanas Digitales Turísticas
Gamified mobile experiences for discovering local heritage, history and culture.
¿Sabías que…?
Stories and local resources shared through the web, QR codes and interactive elements.

I lead the technical side of these products and work on them end to end: architecture, development, prioritisation, validation, security and production deployment.

Recent work includes evolving the admin panel built with Laravel and Filament: project and permission management, multilingual content, metrics and exports.

An AI tourist assistant

We are also building an AI tourist assistant. Visitors ask in natural language about heritage, food, routes, events or services, and the answers are grounded in information provided and validated by the destination, not in open web searches.

I work on its definition, architecture and development. On the AI side, I focus on information retrieval, model comparison and answer evaluation, and I analyse what visitors ask, answer quality, cost and latency to guide improvements.
